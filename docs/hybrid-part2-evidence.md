## Forty-view deadline investigation

The earlier failed isolated run opened forty views in 32.023s and 47.854s,
with slowest openings of 2.055s and 2.973s; the second mutation exceeded its
unchanged five-second deadline. Re-running the **same unchanged alpha.23 test
binary** with a fresh HEDDLE_HOME passed both rounds: openings took 8.724s and
9.129s, with slowest openings of 262ms and 313ms. The isolated test passed in
83.61s. The unchanged alpha.24 baseline also passed both deadlines (93.15s).

After adapting the device carriers, all 14 device-RPC scenarios passed together
in 86.83s, including the forty-view deadline. This evidence supports load
flakiness; the mutation deadline and watcher implementation were not widened.
Logs: `/tmp/hybrid-p2-v23-device-rerun.log`,
`/tmp/hybrid-p2-v23-device-control.log`,
`/tmp/hybrid-p2-v24-device-baseline.log` and
`/tmp/hybrid-p2-v24-device-adaptation-tests.log`.

On the combined Part 1b security fixes and alpha.27 source, the same five-second
control exposed response scheduling behind forty observers' synchronous version
reads. Tracing measured mutations at about 0.55 seconds, with response resumption
at 2.3–2.6 seconds. Yielding between current authority validation and version
reads reduced traced response resumption to 0.8–1.3 seconds. Every current
authority, disclosure, clock and version check remains; the original assertions
and five-second deadline are unchanged. Temporary tracing was removed before
the final source gate.
